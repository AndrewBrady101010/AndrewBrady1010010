# Hi, I'm Andrew 👋

I work at the intersection of clinical expertise, data science, payment integrity, and strategic product thinking.

This GitHub is my public workspace for documenting the rapid evolution of product management in the agentic AI era and my exploration for how these new tools can accelerate the research, prototyping, iteration, and execution speed beyond anything we've seen before.

It's where I'll show my process: what I build, what I test, what I discard, and what proves worth taking further.

## 🎯 Why this GitHub exists

Product work is undeniably changing at a rapid pace across every domain.

The gap between identifying a problem and standing up a working prototype keeps shrinking. That shift changes how ideas get explored, how decisions get made and tested, and when the formal PRD work actually belongs in the process.

I created this GitHub to make this shift towards rapid prototyping and discovery professionally visible as a record of my development.

This is where I'll show my:

- 🧪 Experiments
- 🔬 Prototypes
- 🧭 Decisions
- 🛑 Dead ends
- 🔁 Iterations
- 💎 The few ideas that earn deeper investment

## 🛠️ What I'm focused on

I'm using this space to document what I've learned and what works:
